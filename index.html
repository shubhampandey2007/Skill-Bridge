THE PROBLEM

Students know what they want. They don't always know where they stand.

SkillBridge turns uncertainty into a measurable starting point and a clear direction.

Students often learn multiple technologies without knowing how strong their actual understanding is.

02

No Clear Roadmap

Skill ≠ Certificate
